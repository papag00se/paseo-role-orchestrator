Editorial 3D papercraft meets clean technical illustration, restrained midnight ink background, tactile paper and frosted-glass materials, fine soft studio lighting, beautifully balanced professional composition, generous negative space, coherent with a suite of developer-tool illustrations. Violet and cyan highlights. Communicate reusable specialist roles and coordinated parent-child delegation. One landscape image approximately 3:1 composition. No words, no letters, no logos, no UI screenshot, no watermark.
